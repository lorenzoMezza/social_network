# Terms in short

The same things as the full text, in a few lines. If anything here seems to say something different, the full text is the one that applies.

## The basics

- You must be 18 or older.
- One personal account each, with true information. Do not pretend to be someone else.
- The app is free. The only paid thing is promoting events, which is optional.

## Meeting people

- We do not check who users are, and we are not at meetings or events.
- Events are organized by users, who are responsible for them.
- Meet in public places and tell someone you trust where you are going. If you are in danger, call **112**.
- Positions on the map can be inaccurate. Do not rely on them in an emergency.

## Your content

- What you post stays yours. We use it only to run the app and to show it to the people you share it with.
- Post only what you have the right to post, and photos of other people only with their permission.

## Promotions and credits

- A promotion is paid in credits, 24 hours at a time. Before starting it you see the price and the most you can spend.
- You can stop it at any time: the time already paid stays on and does not come back.
- If you cancel or shorten the event, the time not used comes back as credits, except the first 6 hours.
- Credits do not expire, cannot be transferred and do not turn into money. Deleting your account loses them.

## Not allowed

- Breaking the Community Guidelines.
- Following or tracking people, or trying to find someone's exact position.
- Collecting other users' data or using automated tools.
- Commercial use without our written permission, other than organizing and promoting events in the ways the app provides.

## When something goes wrong

- Report or block anyone from the app. Anyone, even without an account, can report illegal content to [legal@DOMAIN].
- We can remove content and suspend or close accounts that break the rules or the law. We tell you what we decided and why, and you can contest it.

## Leaving

- You can delete your account at any time in Settings.
- If we ever close the service, we tell you at least 30 days before, so you can download your data.

## Disputes

- Italian law applies. As a consumer you keep your rights and can go to the court where you live.
- Write to us first at [support@DOMAIN]: most problems are solved quickly.
