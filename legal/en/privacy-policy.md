## 3. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

### 3.1 Your account

- **Data:** the identifier and the email address of the account you sign in with, the sign-in method, the date your account was created.
- **Why:** to create your account, let you sign in and keep you signed in securely.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Provider:** sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (see section 6).

### 3.2 Your profile and avatar

- **Data:** your name, your @username, your bio, your profile picture, the style of your profile page, your 3D avatar (body shape, colours and the items it wears).
- **Why:** to show who you are to the people you meet, connect with or see on the map.
- **Who sees it:** other users of the app. Your name, @username, picture and avatar are visible to people who see you on the map, in events or in chats. Your story (the events you organize, join and review), your list of connections and the fact that you are going to an event are visible only to your connections, unless you choose everyone in Settings; the organizer of an event always sees who joined it.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Pictures you upload are re-encoded on our server: the metadata stored in the original file (for example the place where a photo was taken) is removed.

### 3.3 Your location

The app shows a map around you. This uses your device's location **only while the app is open** ("while using the app" permission). It never asks for background location.

- **On your device:** your location is used to draw the map and your avatar. Your device's motion sensors (steps, turning) make your avatar walk and turn; this data never leaves your device.
- **Sharing your position with people nearby (your choice):** you choose how people see you with the switch at the top of the map:
  - **Visible:** people within about 1 km who share their position too see your avatar where you are (those who chose Approximate see only your area, as you see theirs). Where it is crowded, each person sees at most 25 people, their connections first, then the nearest: the app shows you how many see you.
  - **Approximate:** people see only a point within about 300 m of you, with no direction or speed. It does not follow your movements: it stays the same while you are in the same area, so it cannot be used to work out where exactly you are.
  - **Invisible:** nobody sees you and you see nobody. Your position is not sent to us for the map; to find the events around you the app sends only a rounded point (about 100 m).

  People you blocked, or who blocked you, never see you.
  
  When Visible or Approximate, the app sends us your position, its accuracy, your direction, speed and whether you are walking, at most once per second. **We keep only your latest position, in memory, never on disk and never as a history.** We stop sharing it, and stop showing you the people around, one minute after you leave the map for another page of the app; at once if you go Invisible, or if the app goes to the background or is closed. If the connection is lost without warning it is forgotten 45 seconds after the last update.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give the first time you choose Visible or Approximate, after the app explains what it means. We record when you gave it and which text you read. You can withdraw it at any time by choosing Invisible; withdrawal does not affect what happened before. By default the app always opens as Invisible: your position is shared only after you choose otherwise (Settings, "Invisible when the app opens", lets you resume your last choice instead).
- **Events:** when you create an event, its place is the one you choose; it is shown to the people the event is visible to.
- **Connections:** when you connect with someone in person (by QR code), we record when it happened and the general place, if you share it, so that you both remember where you met. Between the two phones passes only a one-time code valid for a few seconds, never your name or your account. When someone who sees you on the map taps "Connect" on you, you receive an invitation that you can accept or ignore; it connects nobody by itself.
- **Weather:** to show the weather on the map, your device asks a weather service (see section 6) for the forecast at your position **rounded to about 1 km**.
- **Map tiles:** your device downloads the map's drawings (map tiles) from our servers, which therefore receive your IP address and the area of the map you are looking at. We use them only to send you the map.

### 3.4 People, chats and events

- **Data:** the people you are connected with, the people you block, the messages you send and receive (direct chats, groups and event chats) and when you read them, group pictures, the events you create, join, save, rate and comment on, the photos you post in event albums, the gestures and short messages you send to people on the map.
- **Why:** to provide the social features of the app.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Please note:** messages are stored on our server so that they reach your devices and the people you write to. They are protected in transit and access to them is restricted, but they are **not end-to-end encrypted**. Gestures and short messages sent on the map (the speech bubbles) are delivered to the people nearby and are not stored.
- **Photos of other people:** if you post photos in which other people can be recognised, you must have their permission (see the Terms of Use). Anyone shown in a photo can ask for its removal with the report "I'm in it and don't want it shared" or by writing to us.

### 3.5 Safety, reports and abuse prevention

- **Data:** reports you send or that are sent about you or your content (reason, details, date), blocks, the number of actions you perform in a short time (for example messages sent), technical records of requests to our server (IP address, date, time, request, result).
- **Why:** to keep the app safe, enforce the Terms of Use and Community Guidelines, prevent spam and abuse, protect our systems, and defend legal claims.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest and that of the other users in a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); compliance with legal obligations where we must act on illegal content or answer competent authorities (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). You can object to processing based on legitimate interest (section 8).

### 3.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token to send you the notifications you choose in Settings (events you organize or join, new connections, messages). **Legal basis:** performance of the contract and your choice in your device's settings, which you can change at any time. [Push notifications are not yet active in this version.]

### 3.7 Data stored on your device

The app stores on your device what it needs to work: your sign-in session (in the device's secure storage), your settings, a copy of your profile and avatar so the app opens quickly, and a cache of map tiles (up to 250 MB, removed after 30 days). This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, so no consent is needed (Art. 122 of the Privacy Code). The app uses **no cookies, no advertising identifiers, no analytics and no third-party tracking**.

### 3.8 Promotions, credits and purchases

Optional: only if you promote an event or buy credits.

- **Data:** your balance of credits and its movements (purchases, promotion time paid, credits given back), the promotions of your events (radius, price, dates), and from the stores the transaction identifier, the product and a code tying the purchase to your account. **We neither receive nor store your card or payment method:** the payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play as independent controllers.
- **Why:** to sell credits and credit them once and only to the right account, to charge for promotions, to handle refunds.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); accounting and tax obligations, where they apply (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
- **Sponsored events:** they are shown to people using the app based only on the distance from where they are, marked "Sponsored", with who pays and how far they are shown (Art. 26 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065). We use no profiles and no sensitive data to choose them.

## 9. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** your data and receive a copy: Settings → "Download my data";
- **rectify** it: edit your profile at any time;
- **erase** it: Settings → "Delete account";
- **data portability**: the download is a structured, machine-readable file (JSON);
- **restrict** processing, in the cases provided by Art. 18;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21);
- **withdraw your consent** to sharing your location at any time, by choosing Invisible.

You can also exercise your rights by writing to [privacy@DOMAIN]. We answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why). We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place. In Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).

## 10. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted connections, verified sign-in tokens, restricted access, limits against abuse, checks on uploaded files, and keeping your position only in memory. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will tell you without undue delay (Art. 34 GDPR).
