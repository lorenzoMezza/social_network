# Community Guidelines

Last updated: [DATE]

[APP NAME] is about meeting real people in real places. These rules keep it safe and pleasant for everyone. They are part of the Terms of Use: breaking them can lead to content being removed and accounts being suspended or closed.

## Be yourself

- Use your real name or the name people know you by. Do not impersonate other people, brands or organizations.
- One account per person. You must be 18 or older.

## Respect people

- No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking or intimidation, online or in person.
- No hate speech or discrimination based on ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other protected characteristic.
- No sexual content or unwanted sexual advances. Nothing involving minors, ever: we report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.
- No violent or graphic content, and nothing that encourages self-harm.

## Respect people's privacy

- Do not share other people's personal information (address, phone number, documents) without their permission.
- Post photos in which other people can be recognised only with their permission. If someone asks you to remove a photo of them, remove it.
- Do not follow or track anyone, and do not try to find out where someone is when they chose to show only their area or to be invisible.

## Keep events real

- Events must be real, with a true place and time. Cancel an event you will not hold.
- No events for illegal activities. Organizers are responsible for their events, including permits, safety and the law.
- No scams, pyramid schemes, or selling through events without our permission.

## No spam

- No mass messages, repeated unwanted messages, fake engagement, or misleading links.

## Meet safely

- Meet in public places. Tell someone you trust where you are going. Leave if you feel uncomfortable.
- In an emergency, call **112**.

## Report and block

- Use "Report" on a profile, event, photo or message, and "Block" to stop someone from finding you, messaging you or connecting with you.
- Anyone shown in a photo can ask for its removal with the report "I'm in it and don't want it shared".
- We review reports with a human, tell the people concerned what we decided and why, and let them contest it. Reports are confidential: we do not tell the person you reported who reported them.
