# Privacy in short

The same things as the full text, in a few lines. If anything here seems to say something different, the full text is the one that applies.

## Your location

- The app uses it only while it is open, never in the background.
- You choose who sees you with the switch at the top of the map:
  - **Visible**: people within about 1 km see where you are.
  - **Approximate**: they see only your area, about 300 m wide.
  - **Invisible**: nobody sees you and your position is not sent to us.
- The app opens as Invisible, unless you switch that off in Settings.
- We keep only your latest position, in memory, for 45 seconds. Never a history.
- Your steps and turns, measured by your phone, stay on your phone.

## What other people see

- Your name, @username, picture, bio and avatar.
- Your events, photos and messages, only to the people they are meant for.
- People you block never see you.

## What we never do

- Sell your data.
- Use analytics or tracking tools, or show you ads based on a profile of you. Events sponsored by their organizers are marked as such and depend only on distance.

## Messages and photos

- Messages are stored on our server so they reach you. They are protected in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted.
- Photos you upload lose their hidden data, such as the place where they were taken.
- Post photos of other people only with their permission. Anyone in a photo can ask us to remove it.

## Promotions and credits

- Only if you promote an event: we keep your balance and the movements of your credits. Payment is handled by the App Store and Google Play: we never see your card.

## Other services

- The map comes from our servers, the weather from an outside service: both see your IP address and the area you look at. The weather service gets your position rounded to about 1 km.

## How long we keep things

- Your account and profile: until you delete them.
- Event chats: 30 days after the event ends. Events: up to 12 months after they end.
- Reports: 12 months.

## Your rights

- Get a copy of your data: Settings → "Download my data".
- Delete everything: Settings → "Delete account".
- Questions and requests: [privacy@DOMAIN]. We answer within a month.
- You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

You must be 18 or older to use the app.
