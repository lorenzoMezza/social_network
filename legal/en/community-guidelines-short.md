# The rules in short

The same things as the full text, in a few lines. If anything here seems to say something different, the full text is the one that applies.

- **Be yourself.** Your real name, one account, 18 or older.
- **Respect people.** No harassment, threats, hate or unwanted sexual advances. Nothing involving minors, ever.
- **Respect privacy.** No one's personal information or recognisable photo without their permission. Remove a photo if the person in it asks you to.
- **Don't follow anyone.** No tracking, and no trying to find people who show only their area or are invisible.
- **Real events only.** A true place and time. Cancel what you will not hold. Nothing illegal.
- **No spam or scams.** No mass messages, fake engagement, misleading links or selling.
- **Meet safely.** Public places, tell someone where you are going. In an emergency, call **112**.
- **Report and block.** Use "Report" and "Block" on anything wrong. Reports are confidential.

Breaking these rules can get content removed and accounts suspended or closed.
